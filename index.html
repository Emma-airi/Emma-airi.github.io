About Me

A first-class Aerospace Engineer who likes to check a model against the real thing: by hand calculation, by test and by reporting plainly where it falls short

I've led the engineering of a student CubeSat through its preliminary and critical design reviews. I worked on the thermal design of the EXCALIBUR stratospheric balloon payload and wrote its thermal vacuum test protocol. On an Airbus Defence and Space-backed research project, our four-person team measured wing vibration with 3D scanning and finite element analysis, then presented the work at the ICMAE conference.

At the Francis Crick Institute's Making Lab, I spent nine weeks developing thermoplastic microfluidic fabrication, from resin moulds to hot embossing and ultrasonic welding. Along the way I've debugged an inherited Fortran solver, written my own orbit propagator in Python and learnt that two models disagreeing is information rather than an inconvenience.

I'm working towards CEng with the IMechE and the RAeS. I'm looking for graduate roles in mechanical design, GNC or assembly, integration and test (AIT) across aviation, space and defence. I'm always keen to talk with people building things that fly or orbit.
